## Post Copy

Here's what 24 hours of building looks like.

This is PanelForge — a synthetic focus group simulator that turns internet opinions into structured, evidence-backed consumer insights.

[VIDEO]

In this demo you'll see:
- Creating a new research project in seconds
- Ingesting real data from Reddit, YouTube, and product reviews
- Watching AI cluster opinions into distinct consumer personas
- A moderated two-round panel discussion between those personas
- A full analytics dashboard with consensus scores, risk flags, and evidence-linked quotes

Built at the RUIEEE Buildathon (Rutgers) in 24 hours by a team of 3.

**Tech stack:**
- Python + FastAPI (backend orchestration)
- Google Gemini 2.5 Flash (structured AI generation)
- ChromaDB + Leiden algorithm (persona clustering)
- React 19 + Tailwind + Recharts (visualization dashboard)

**What's next:**
- Calibrating personas against real market research outcomes
- Adding more data sources (App Store reviews, Twitter/X, Amazon)
- Exploring integration with product management tools

This week I broke down the project across 6 posts — from the hackathon experience, to the market gap we identified, to UI design, data engineering, AI orchestration, and development tools.

Thanks to everyone who engaged, commented, and reached out this week. The conversations have been incredible.

If you're building something — ship it. Share it. The worst that happens is you learn something.

- Upload natively to LinkedIn (not YouTube link — native gets 5x reach)
