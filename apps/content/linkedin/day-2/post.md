## Post Copy

"How do you know if people actually want this?"

That's the question every founder dreads. And the answer usually involves:
- $15K+ for a focus group
- 4-6 weeks of lead time
- Recruiting bias you can't control
- Results that are often too late to act on

At the RUIEEE Buildathon, my team asked: what if you could simulate a focus group in 60 seconds?

Not fake opinions. Not generic AI responses.

Real consumer sentiment, extracted from Reddit threads, YouTube reviews, and product forums — clustered into personas that actually represent how people think and talk about your product.

That's PanelForge.

Here's how it works:
1. You give it a product concept and a few seed URLs
2. It ingests real consumer opinions from the internet
3. It clusters them into 4-5 distinct personas using graph-based community detection
4. It runs a moderated two-round panel discussion between those personas
5. You get a dashboard with consensus scores, risk flags, and evidence-backed quotes

Every single insight traces back to a real source. No hallucination. No guessing.

We identified a gap: early-stage founders and PMs iterate faster than research can keep up. By the time traditional research comes back, you've already shipped V2.

PanelForge gives you directional signal in minutes, not months.
