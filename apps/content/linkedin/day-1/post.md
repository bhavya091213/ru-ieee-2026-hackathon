## Post Copy

24 hours. One idea. Zero sleep.

Last weekend I competed in the RUIEEE Buildathon at Rutgers, and it was one of the best hackathon experiences I've had.

There's something electric about being in a room full of people who all decided to sacrifice their weekend to build something from scratch. No tutorials. No hand-holding. Just an idea and a deadline.

Our team of 3 came in without a plan. We left with a working product that I'm genuinely proud of — an AI-powered synthetic focus group simulator called PanelForge.

But more on the technical side later this week.

What I want to highlight today is the energy of this event. The conversations between teams. The 2 AM debugging sessions where strangers became collaborators. The moment at hour 18 when everything finally clicked.

If you were at the Buildathon — drop a comment. I'd love to connect.

And if you've never done a hackathon: this is your sign. The best way to learn is to build something under pressure with people who push you.
